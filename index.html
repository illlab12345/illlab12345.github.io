01 / About

Research at the intersection of reasoning and responsibility.

I'm Haozengran Wang, an undergraduate student in Big Data Management and Applied Data Science at Harbin Institute of Technology and emlyon business school. My research focuses on how large language models reason, use tools, and act reliably.

During my undergraduate studies, I have been fortunate to pursue research on LLM reasoning under the guidance of Prof. Amy Wenxuan Ding at emlyon business school. I have also had the opportunity to conduct research as an intern at the Artificial Intelligence Research Institute, HKU Business School, under the guidance of Prof. Hailiang Chen.

Beyond this work, I explore reliable agentic systems and applied machine learning, from quantitative factor discovery to clinical prediction and agricultural risk monitoring.
